# MELTDOWN

A nuclear reactor control-room simulator. You are the night-shift operator of a
pressurized water reactor: start it from cold, take it critical, put megawatts
on the grid, and keep yourself together while you do it.

**▶ Play: https://jasraajdevz.github.io/meltdown/**

Works in any modern browser, desktop or phone. Your progress saves locally.

### What you're actually doing

- **Take the plant up.** Coolant pumps first — their heat warms the loop before
  you ever go critical. Then pressure, then withdraw the rods, then dilute boron
  until the reactor comes alive. Watch the startup rate.
- **Follow the dispatcher.** The grid asks for a load in megawatts and changes it
  through the watch. Sitting on target pays; ignoring it doesn't.
- **Burn fuel.** Uranium is both your fuel and your pay. Burnup climbs, reactivity
  falls, and eventually you have to schedule a refuelling outage.
- **Stay sane.** The shift is long, the alarms are loud, and something in the
  turbine hall screams. Keep the canteen stocked.

You are never on a timer. The protection system trips before the plant breaks,
a trip is survivable, and END SHIFT always banks your work — losing the core
takes real neglect.

Press the 📖 button in the control room for the full operator's manual.
